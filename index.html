Why study here

A modern technical school built to give young people across the Sepik region real-world skills and a path to work close to home.

  • Regional access

    Serves students from both East and West Sepik.

  • Beside the Amaku River

    A calm place to learn, 20 minutes on foot from Newtown.

  • Hands-on learning

    Competency-based trade training, not just theory.

  • Job-ready pathways

    Skills to find work or start a small business.

  • Affordable and local

    Train at home instead of paying to move to a big city.
